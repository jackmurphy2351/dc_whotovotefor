# About
## Democratic Candidate for At-Large Member of the Council of the District of Columbia
Dear Neighbors, 

I’ve lived in DC my entire life, and today I am an attorney raising a family here in Ward 7. This city shaped who I am, and my career has been focused on work that affects people’s everyday quality of life – especially housing stability, education, public safety, and access to essential services.

I’m a proud graduate of Howard University, where I earned my undergraduate degree in political science with honors and later my law degree. While in school, I worked with Housing Counseling Services, helping low-income tenants understand their rights and avoid losing their homes, and at a DC law firm, where I saw firsthand how policy decisions show up in people’s real lives.

During law school, I worked at AARP, assisting seniors facing foreclosure tied to property tax issues and reverse mortgages. That experience showed me how easily people can fall through the cracks – and how important it is for the government to be practical, accessible, and responsive, especially for older residents. I also had the opportunity to study abroad in South Africa and intern at the country’s largest law firm, which broadened my perspective on justice, governance, and public service.

​After being admitted to practice law in DC in 2012, I built a legal practice focused on housing, landlord-tenant issues, real estate, and personal injury. Most recently, I spent four years serving at the DC Council as Committee and Policy Director for At-Large Councilmember Anita Bonds, helping lead legislative strategy, agency oversight, and policy implementation affecting residents across all eight wards. 

Over the years, I’ve also served in a number of civic leadership roles in DC, including as President of the DC Young Democrats, a board member of the United Planning Organization, a member of the DC Democratic State Committee, and currently as DC’s National Committeeman and member of the Democratic National Committee.

Outside of work, my family and my community shape how I see the world and the responsibilities of public service. I’m a member of St. Timothy’s Episcopal Church and a proud husband and dad. My wife, Ashley, and I are raising our two children, Kendall and Cameron, with a personal commitment to the future of this city and the next generation.

I’m running for DC Council At-Large because I believe our city needs experienced leadership ready to deliver on day one – leadership that understands how policy turns into real results for residents. I want to build on my experience with a steady, practical approach focused on safe streets, affordable housing, strong starts for children, economic opportunity for working families, dignity and support for seniors, and a city government that works clearly and efficiently for everyone.

I would be honored to earn your vote for DC Council At-Large.

Kevin Chavous
# Platform
## A PLATFORM FOCUSED ON  EVERYDAY QUALITY OF LIFE
I’m running for DC Council At-Large because this city is home to my family – and because Washingtonians deserve leadership focused on improving everyday quality of life. **Throughout my career, I’ve worked on the practical business of city government and public policy, focused on solving real problems and making government work better for the people it serves**. 

My experience spans safer streets, affordable housing, early education and school readiness, workforce and economic opportunity, support for seniors, and the day-to-day operations of city government. Everything I bring to this race is grounded in experience, clear-eyed oversight, and a commitment to delivering results.

This platform outlines my priorities and approach, but it’s not the end of the conversation. **I believe the best solutions come from listening and working together**.
​
Let’s get to work.
## SAFER STREETS
Safe streets are the foundation of strong neighborhoods. Washingtonians deserve to feel secure in their neighborhoods while knowing that public safety efforts are smart, fair, and effective. Kevin’s approach focuses on enforcement that works, prevention that matters, and services that stop harm before it happens.
- Support smart, effective enforcement that focuses on violent crime and repeat offenders
- Strengthen community policing to rebuild trust and improve neighborhood-level safety
- Expand targeted mental health and crisis-response services to prevent violence before it occurs
- Coordinate youth diversion, workforce, and support programs to intervene early and reduce recidivism
## AFFORDABLE HOUSING
Housing stability is essential for families, seniors, and workers to stay and thrive in DC. Kevin’s housing priorities focus on increasing supply, protecting residents, and helping people build long-term stability in the neighborhoods they call home.
- Increase affordable housing supply through zoning updates, ADUs, and adaptive reuse of vacant properties 
- Strengthen programs that help first-time and longtime homeowners stay in their homes
- Expand permanent supportive housing and targeted rental assistance for vulnerable residents    
- Protect tenants while ensuring housing laws are enforced clearly and consistently
## EARLY EDUCATION  & SCHOOL READINESS
Strong schools start before the first day of class. Kevin believes every child should enter school ready to learn, with the support needed to succeed from day one. Early investment pays lifelong dividends – for families and for the District.
- Expand early childhood education and school-readiness programs citywide 
- Ensure students begin the school year healthy by supporting access to medical and dental screenings
- Give schools and educators the flexibility to address low achievement and student needs
- Align education spending with outcomes so investments deliver real results for students
## WORKFORCE & ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY
Economic opportunity allows families and communities to thrive. Kevin believes DC’s growth should create real pathways to good jobs, strong local businesses, and long-term stability for residents in every ward. His approach connects workforce training, worker protections, and neighborhood investment so that growth benefits the people who live here.
- Expand job training, apprenticeships, and career pipelines tied to high-demand fields, including construction, healthcare, and infrastructure 
- Strengthen First Source and local hiring requirements so DC residents benefit directly from major development projects
- Protect workers by enforcing wage, safety, and labor standards and holding bad actors accountable
- Invest in neighborhood-based economic development, including small businesses, BIDs, and commercial-to-residential revitalization
## SUPPORTING SENIORS & AGING WITH DIGNITY
Seniors built this city – and DC must ensure they can age with dignity, security, and independence. Kevin’s priorities focus on protecting seniors, simplifying access to services, and helping older residents remain safely in their homes and communities.
- Expand property tax relief and housing supports so seniors can age in place 
- Strengthen protections against fraud, exploitation, and predatory practices targeting seniors
- Improve access to benefits and services by simplifying enrollment and delivery
- Support nutrition, transportation, and community-based programs that reduce isolation
## PROTECTING CIVIL RIGHTS & DC HOME RULE
DC has long stood as a place where diversity, opportunity, and freedom are core values. Kevin believes our city must continue to lead in protecting civil rights, ensuring equal protection under the law, and defending the right of DC residents to govern themselves. He will work to strengthen protections for vulnerable communities, stand against discrimination in all forms, and defend DC’s Home Rule from federal interference.
- Protect and strengthen civil rights laws so that every person, regardless of race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or immigration status, is treated with dignity, fairness, and equal protection under the law.
- Support policies that protect the civil rights of our Latino and immigrant communities, keep families together, ensure access to legal representation, and create fair and humane pathways to stability for families who contribute to our neighborhoods and economy.
- Strengthen protections against hate crimes and bias, including violence and discrimination targeting LGBTQ+ residents, and support community-based initiatives that build safer and more inclusive neighborhoods.
- Stand up for the right of District residents to govern themselves by defending DC Home Rule and pushing back against federal interference from Congress or the White House.
## A GOVERNMENT THAT WORKS
City government should work clearly, efficiently, and transparently for the people it serves. Kevin’s experience inside DC Council oversight gives him a practical understanding of what works, what doesn’t, and how to fix it – without delay.
- Strengthen agency oversight to ensure laws are implemented as intended 
- Improve service delivery by fixing bottlenecks and outdated processes
- Require clear standards and accountability in inspections, enforcement, and permitting
- Use technology responsibly to improve efficiency while protecting residents from fraud and abuse
