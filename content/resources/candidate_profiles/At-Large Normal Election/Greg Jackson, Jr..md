# About
## National Leader. Local Commitment.
**Greg Jackson was shot and nearly killed in the District in 2013. Since that tragic night, he has become a leading advocate, organizer and after leading efforts to pass the first federal law on gun violence in 30 years, he was appointed Special Assistant to President Joe Biden on Gun Violence Prevention.**

**In this role, he led a 49% reduction in homicides nationwide in just 3 years.**

 A community organizer at heart, Greg Jackson has organized and served in Washington, D.C. since 2007 where he helped found and later serve as the co-chair of DC for Obama, a movement that deployed thousands of volunteers to achieve victory in the historic 2008 election of President Barack Obama. Greg was later hired by the Democratic National Committee to lead a team of over 380 staff in North Carolina, where he played a pivotal role leading over 36 local campaigns. After Barack Obama was re-elected in 2012, Greg returned to DC as the national field director of the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee, where he led the largest midterm voter registration effort in U.S. History. **It was then that he survived a near fatal gunshot wound, which led him to shift from campaigns to making D.C. safer place to call home**.

In 2015, he was appointed to the cabinet of Mayor Muriel Bowser as her first Director of Community Relations and Services, where he oversaw an effort to successfully resolve over 2,000 resident concerns. During the 2016 election, Greg was hired as the first national field director of "For Our Future", an organization built and funded by national labor unions to increase voter turnout in Black and Brown communities. After the challenging 2016 election, Greg returned to DC government where he joined the Department of Parks and Recreation (DPR) as their Communication and Community Relations Director where he drove historic numbers of residents to our District's incredible parks and recreation centers in the spirit of "play". It was here he coined DPR's motto "Where Fun Happens", launched the award winning "FitDC" campaign and enhanced opportunities for DC youth, residents and seniors to enjoy the District through recreation.

**After losing a family friend to gun violence in 2019, Greg returned to the national stage to address gun violence across the country**. He was hired as National Advocacy Director and Executive Director for [Community Justice Action Fund](https://www.cjactionfund.org/), the nation's only Black survivor led advocacy organization focused on ending gun violence. While at Community Justice, Greg was able to lead advocacy work to ensure federal funds responding to the COVID-19 pandemic (American Rescue Plan) prioritized public safety, led battles to secure $42 billion in funding for gun violence prevention and pass the first law on gun violence in 30 years, the Bipartisan Safer Communities Act. After the passage of this historic bill, Greg was appointed as Special Assistant to President Biden on Gun Violence and named the Deputy Director of the first ever White House Office of Gun Violence Prevention. At the service of the President, Greg developed and implemented 54 executive actions, coordinated federal responses to mass shootings , oversaw the allocation of billions and fully implemented the Bipartisan Safer Communities Act, leading to a 49% reduction in homicides across the United States of America.

**After his time at the White House, Greg is working on getting his Master's in Public Health from Yale University, serving as the President of the Rocket Foundation alongside music artist Quavo and consulting with organizations across the country to make communities safer from gun violence.**

Despite his dynamic progress, Greg has lost multiple loved ones to gun violence and his D.C. home was hit 13 times during a nearby shooting by while he served in the White House. As Council member, Greg aspires to bring his dynamic experience and commitment to making DC safer from gun violence and a place where all can thrive.

# Issues
## Affordability and Jobs
### Create Jobs · Lower Everyday Costs · Generate Wealth

DC now has the highest unemployment of any major city in the U.S. due federal layoffs and a shrinking private economy. Additionally, 95% of businesses in DC are small home grown businesses.

My affordability plan is focused on urgent need of today and the evolving economy of tomorrow. It will lower costs, expand opportunities, and help more residents build wealth in the city they love.

We'll increase housing supply, protect longtime residents from displacement, and create stronger pathways to homeownership for working families.

I'll also focus on reducing everyday costs by investing in childcare, transportation, workforce development, and reliable city services.

Instead of relying on constant tax increases, we'll grow D.C.'s economy by supporting small businesses, revitalize downtown, create good-paying jobs, support small businesses and strengthen job training programs.

---
### Greg Jackson's Action Proposals
#### Creating Jobs
**Expand support for small businesses.** My plan is to concentrate DC government resources, relief and oversight on making DC one of the easiest and most rewarding places to operate a small business. This includes relieving the tax burdens on small businesses, clearing challenging logistical hurdles and providing incentives for job creating new businesses to choose DC as their home.

**Strengthen support for our highest employing industry, hospitality.** From restaurants to wellness centers, our city's economy has been centered on hospitality and DC Council should focus on strengthening, expanding and supporting the industry that fuels our economy.

**Attract new businesses through tax and startup cost relief.** Major cities across the country are providing tax relief, fee waivers and grants to attract businesses, DC MUST do the same. Reducing or waiving short term costs to generate more jobs is key.

**Improve job training and workforce pipelines.** While we create opportunity, we must also strengthen our DC workforce to be prepared for the modern economy with stronger hard and soft skills.

**Improve business service delivery.** From restaurants to entrepreneurs, DC has failed to keep up with the necessary pace of service delivery to keep businesses safe, compliant and operational. Through oversight, we must improve our service delivery, turn around times and reduce red tape.

---
#### Lowering the Cost of Living and Generating Wealth
**Increase supply, starting with small housing providers.** Increasing affordable housing supply, starts with optimizing our current housing providers. That means providing more incentives and less burdens for homeowners, multi-unit owners and small businesses to provide long term, midterm and even short term housing. Additionally, we must push for more housing that is truly affordable (not just market rate) while defending necessary protections for both tenants and housing providers.

**Make DC the homebuying assistance leader of the U.S.** "I believe the cheapest home is one that a family owns". I want to make DC the home buying assistance capitol of the U.S., by expanding assistance programs, providing extra incentives for our teachers and resources for repairing, modernizing or preserving our homes. Additionally, we must expand resources or reliefs for repairs, weatherization and solar programs that help offset rising utility costs for home owners.

**Support long term residents and estate planning.** Lastly, we must provide more support to long term residents through repair grants, targeted tax relief and resources to support estate planning.

---
#### Lowering Everyday Costs
**Reducing childcare costs.** Childcare costs have skyrocketed due to increased demand, limited workforce and limited facilities in the District. I will focus on protecting Paid Family Leave, the Early Childhood Educator Pay Equity Fund, Universal Pre-K and expanding resources to support child care facillities and grants.

**Reliable, safer and cheaper transportation options.** Improving means for transportation is a major way to address everday costs. My plan is to strengthen public transit, claw back costly fines for our drivers and improve traffic safety through proactive evidence based behavior change tactics, not just traffic cameras and under maintained traffic experiments.

**Improved DC Government service delivery.** Lastly, we must strengthen everyday city services from agencies like the Department of Public Works to lower the impact of costly interruptions from failed trash pickups, snow removal or overall city maintenance.
## Addressing Youth Violence in DC
#### Did You Know

**95% of all guns** found at crime scenes in DC come from outside of DC.
**Gun violence is the #1 cause of death** in the Black Community and ALL youth.

**$1.5 million dollars** is the estimated cost per homicide in DC.

**Greg Jackson, candidate for DC Council,** is a leading national expert on preventing gun violence and violent crime.

**For over 12 years, Greg Jackson, a survivor of gun violence,** has led policy change to end gun violence. Most recently, he served as the Deputy Director of the White House Office of Gun Violence Prevention under President Joe Biden and Vice President Kamala Harris.

He led the creation of policies and secured billions in resources to prevent violence, leading to a 49% decrease in homicides in just three years.

---
### Greg's Action Proposals

#### Establish Youth Network for Safety
Create a network of youth impacted, concerned or at risk to violent crime to advise public safety policy to address violence among and against youth.
#### Expand Cognitive Behavior and School Based Mental Health Programs
Short term goal oriented mental therapy that takes a hands-on approach to change patterns of thinking or behavior. These strategies have been incredibly impactful in DC and similar cities battling community violence.
#### Establish Youth Safety Fund
Initially funded by public-private partnerships, this fund will be directed by youth to support youth centered initiatives to address violence and root causes.
#### Create a Safe Nights DC Initiative
Expanding late night youth targeted recreation, engagement and restorative activities focused on high youth traffic areas.
#### Provide Wrap Around Services for Our Most Vulnerable Families
Targeted resources will work across generations to support families plagued with trauma or violent interactions.
#### Strengthen Community Violence Intervention
Through increased funding, training and oversight we can create a thriving ecosystem of evidence based programs to prevent violence before it happens.

---
#### Enhance Crime Gun Intelligence
Strengthening law enforcement strategies to intercept guns and clear cases, we should add more resources to identify the source of illegal guns and investigative tools, not just patrol or surveillance.
#### Regional Gun Trafficking Initiative
Partnering with leaders in Virginia and Maryland, establish an interstate initiative to shut down the "Iron Pipeline" by disrupting gun trafficking rings in the DMV.
#### Establish Gun Violence Trauma Recovery Centers
Trauma Recovery Centers use proven approaches to help crime survivors meet their needs and care across systems.
#### Gun Violence Crisis Response
Mass shootings and surges in violence require a response that will coordinate across agencies and support victims, survivors, and those present but not injured.
#### Firearm Safe Storage
This initiative will further educate gun owners on safe storage and strengthen accountability measures to ensure firearms are safely stored or reported lost/stolen.

---
#### Group Violence Reduction Strategy
The strategy works by engaging directly with those most intimately involved in and affected by violence, leveraging an intentional collaboration between law enforcement, social services, and community members.
#### Strengthen Out of School Programs and Services
DC's out of school programs are key to minimize unsuperviced gatherings and ensure safe, constructive, recreational time for our youth. Strengthening these efforts in communities most vulnerable are key to educational, economic and safety outcomes.
#### Violent Crime + Resources Dashboard
This first of its kind resource will better showcase violent crime data from both health and law enforcement sources, as well as layering the community resources available to communities impacted by violence.
#### Enhanced Victim Services
Families of those lost and victims suffering from injury require more services, as well as reformed criteria to achieve equitable access for families and survivors of violent crime.
## Strengthening Education in DC
### Schools Families Can Trust
D.C. has strong educators and dedicated families. In fact, only 5 states and DC have recovered from the COVID drop, but too many parents are still unsure whether the system is delivering as it should.

Greg Jackson has worked across city governments and national youth-serving systems to connect families to services and improve outcomes in every ward.

This platform focuses on measurable improvements families can feel.

This plan strengthens the foundations students need from cradle to career: literacy, attendance, tutoring access, school climate, special education improvements and strong family partnerships.

Every recommendation includes public reporting, measurable benchmarks, and accountability families can track school-by-school.

---
### Greg Jackson's Action Proposals
#### Safe & Reliable School Buildings
Every student deserves to learn in a safe, modern building.

I will strengthen oversight of the Department of General Services to improve maintenance, ensure safety issues like broken locks are addressed quickly, and support modernization planning that keeps students close to their school communities during renovations.

I will also work to ensure charter schools receive equitable facilities support so all students, regardless of school type, have access to safe and well-maintained learning environments.

---
#### Expanding Access to Affordable Childcare

Another issue I hear about all the time from families is childcare. Too many parents are struggling to find available and affordable childcare slots close to home. I'm focused on protecting childcare slots, strengthening the pipeline of early childhood educators through investments in "grow your own" programs, and protecting the Early Childhood Educator Pay Equity Fund. We will push toward a future where childcare costs families no more than 7% of a family's income, because affordable childcare is essential for strong families and strong schools.

---
#### Prepare DC Students for Real Careers and Real Opportunity

Students should graduate ready to succeed in a digital-age economy. I will support investments in digital literacy, expand career-connected learning and internships, and strengthen partnerships between schools, higher education, and employers so students graduate with the skills and experiences they need to thrive.

Real-world readiness should start before graduation, with earlier exposure to careers, mentorship, and hands-on learning opportunities across every ward.

---
#### High Impact Tutoring for Students Who Need It Most
I will protect the budget for high-impact tutoring, requiring public reporting on tutoring access and dosage. I'll prioritize tracking results school-by-school, because if tutoring works, every child who needs it should receive enough of it to matter.

---
#### Strengthen Community Schools Model
I will strengthen community schools by investing in place-based partnerships that connect students and families to health, academic, and social supports beyond the classroom, and by expanding collaboration with trusted community organizations.

---
#### Establish a Citywide Literacy Milestone Framework "We Read DC"
Families deserve clear information about their child's reading progress. I will push for strengthening public reporting on literacy outcomes, support school-level recovery planning when students fall behind, and ensure families receive timely, understandable updates about whether their child is on track, because reading success by the end of elementary school should never be optional.

---
#### Treat Chronic Absenteeism as a Citywide Emergency

Students cannot succeed if they are not in school. I will treat chronic absenteeism as a citywide priority by strengthening school-level attendance recovery planning, improving coordination between schools, transportation, and family support services, and requiring monthly public reporting so families and policymakers can track progress.

---
#### Put School Climate and Belonging Back at the Center of School Quality
Safe, calm, and welcoming schools produce stronger outcomes. I will partner with DCPS, OSSE, and public charter leaders to strengthen school climate reporting alongside academic results, support restorative-practice implementation through targeted investments, and help direct resources toward schools where climate indicators show students need the most support, because joy and order are not opposites. They are partners.

---
#### Supporting Teachers with Housing & Childcare Benefits
Strong schools depend on strong educators. I will support expanding benefits for our teachers, including childcare benefits, access home buying assistance programs specifically for teachers and school staff, so educators can afford to live and work in the communities they serve.

---
#### Expanding Safe Passage and Youth Safety Supports
Students should be safe traveling to and from school and in their neighborhoods. I will support expanding Safe Passage investments, strengthen coordination between schools and violence prevention agencies, and increase access to afterschool, evening and summer opportunities that give youth safe, engaging places to be.

---
#### Reliable Transportation for Students with Special Needs
Special education transportation in DC has faced persistent reliability challenges that place real strain on students and families. I will strengthen oversight of transportation services, improve transparency around performance, and support investments that ensure students arrive safely and on time every day.
