# The Orange Plan is a governing blueprint rooted in execution, not ideology.

It details how an Orange administration will make daily life work for residents, families, businesses, and visitors—by running the city competently, protecting public safety, restoring fiscal strength, and ensuring opportunity is attainable.

1. EDUCATION • WORKFORCE • PUBLIC SAFETY
	- Prepare 3- and 4-year-olds for kindergarten through health, vision, and hearing assessments.
	- Ensure students read independently and can add, subtract, multiply, and divide upon entering 4th grade.
	- Ensure 8th graders are prepared for high school, including algebra, geometry, and advanced English.
	- Guarantee four paths for graduating seniors: UDC tuition-free; four-year college with TAG; apprenticeship; entrepreneurship through DSLBD.
	- Crime is not an option—opportunity is guaranteed and accountability is expected.
2. YOUTH OPPORTUNITY & ACCOUNTABILITY
	- Curfew: Individuals under 18 must be off DC streets Sunday–Thursday, 11:00 PM–6:00 AM, with defined exceptions.
	- Mandate youth apprenticeship programs across all DC agencies and intergovernmental agencies.
	- Scale apprenticeships to match the success of MBSYEP.
3. PUBLIC SAFETY THAT WORKS
	- Support law enforcement while mandating accountability and respect for civil and human rights.
	- Restore and maintain a sworn police force of approximately 4,100 officers.
	- Public safety is foundational to residents’ quality of life and to the 25–30 million visitors who sustain DC’s economy.
4. HEALTH SECURITY THAT WORKS
	- Early identification of vision, hearing, and developmental issues in early childhood.
	- Strengthen emergency preparedness and public health coordination.
	- Protect families, seniors, and vulnerable residents through prevention and readiness.
5. AFFORDABLE HOUSING PEOPLE CAN ATTAIN
	- Expand attainable homeownership and rental opportunities for working families, seniors, and young professionals.
	- Support innovative housing models, including workforce housing and tiny homes for residents earning $50,000 or less.
	- Align housing policy with transit, jobs, and neighborhood stability.
6. ECONOMIC GROWTH WITHOUT RAISING TAXES
	- Bring approved projects in the pipeline to fruition.
	- Stimulate economic activity to grow revenue rather than raising taxes.
	- Circulate DC dollars within the District by strengthening Certified Business Enterprises and local businesses.
	- Support tourism, entertainment, personal services, and small business investment.
7. RESTORING FISCAL STRENGTH & AAA CREDIT
	- Restore DC’s AAA bond rating through disciplined budgeting, strong reserves, and transparent financial management.
	- Align spending with outcomes and long-term fiscal sustainability.
	- Ensure credibility with bond markets, investors, and federal partners.
8. COMMON GROUND WITH THE WHITE HOUSE & CONGRESS
	- Work pragmatically with federal partners to advance shared goals.
	- We will not negotiate in fear, nor fear to negotiate.
	- Advance DC priorities through competence, credibility, and steady leadership.
9. RANKED CHOICE VOTING & CONSENSUS LEADERSHIP
	- RCV rewards collaboration, credibility, and trust.
	- An Orange administration will lead through consensus without sacrificing principles.
	- Voters may not agree on everything—but they deserve confidence the city will run effectively.
10. HOME RULE, AUTONOMY & STATEHOOD
	- Preserve Home Rule and the Home Rule Act of 1973.
	- Protect DC autonomy while continuing to pursue statehood.
	- Defend the rights and dignity of DC residents through competent governance.

An Orange administration will take care of you, your children, visitors, businesses, and ensure the DC dollar circulates within the DC economy. Passion matters—but someone still has to run the city.

# FISCAL DISCIPLINE UNDER ARTICLE I

Revenue Alignment, Taxpayer Relief, and Structural Stability in the District of Columbia

## Executive Summary

###### WASHINGTON, D.C. FACES A DOCUMENTED FISCAL INFLECTION POINT.

According to reporting by The Washington Post (Feb. 11, 2026), Mayor Muriel Bowser warned the D.C. Council that maintaining current services would require approximately $1.1 billion in additional revenue over the financial plan period.

Simultaneously, congressional action denying the District’s tax decoupling legislation results in approximately $600 million in multi-year revenue adjustments within the District’s financial plan.

These figures, taken together, create a structural exposure exceeding $1 billion over the financial plan window.

This document integrates:
- $600M federal tax conformity relief flowing directly to taxpayers
- $1.1B reported service-maintenance gap
- $400M+ estimated healthcare and childcare cost growth
- Constitutional authority under Article I, Section 8
- A structural fiscal repair plan
- A Revenue Alignment Commission proposal

Recent congressional action prevents the District from opting out of certain federal tax provisions. As a result, approximately $593 million–$600 million in multi-year tax relief flows directly to District residents and businesses rather than being retained in the local financial plan.
