# EDUCATION

## Program Overview
We will realign D.C. schools to focus on proven fundamentals like reading, cursive, and arithmetic in early grades, followed by mandatory STEM, vocational training, and college readiness programs. Ending social promotion and preparing every student for real-world success. We aim to fully implement these initiatives by August 2027.
### _Pre-K through 5th Grade - Fundamentals_
#### Reading Excellence
- Every child will **master phonics-based reading** by third grade through daily instruction and practice
- We’ll partner with libraries to **create home reading programs** that engage families in their child’s literacy journey    
- Students who struggle will receive **immediate intervention**, not social promotion to the next grade level.
#### Arithmetic Mastery
- Students will build strong mathematical foundations through sequential skill development and mental math fluency requirements
- **Real-world application projects** will show students how math applies to everyday life
- **Small group instruction** will ensure no child falls behind in these essential skills
#### Cursive Writing Revival
- Cursive writing develops **fine motor skills**, **hand-eye coordination**, and connects students to historical documents they’ll encounter throughout life 
- This art **enhances brain development** and provides students with a unique personal signature    
- Every graduate will leave elementary school able to read and write in cursive
### _6th through 12th Grade - Career and College Preparation_
We're equipping students with the skills and foundation to succeed not only in college applications but in today’s competitive workforce. The initiatives aim to be fully implemented by August 2027.
#### Mandatory STEM Foundation
- All students will complete integrated Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics courses with **hands-on laboratory experience**    
- **Technology literacy certification** will prepare students for modern careers while innovation projects develop problem-solving skills    
#### Vocational Training Tracks
- Students will choose from skilled trades, healthcare, business, government service, or creative **industries pathways** based on their interests and aptitudes 
- Each track includes real **certification opportunities and partnerships** with local employers for **internships and job placement**
#### College Readiness Programs
- **Advanced Placement Courses** to expand and give college-bound students a head start on their higher education
- **College application workshops and scholarship** support to ensure financial barriers won't prevent qualified students from pursuing their dreams
- University partnerships to provide **dual-enrollment opportunities** for exceptional students

- Launch a “**Beautify D.C.**” jobs initiative employing seniors and youth

- Let’s **invest in our people**, not just our buildings.

# PUBLIC SAFTEY

## Program Overview
Every Washingtonian deserves to feel safe in their neighborhood, especially our seniors. Safety is a right, not a privilege, and we will ensure public safety that is fair, accountable, and community driven.
## Community Safety Initiatives

### Traffic and Street Safety
- Enforce regulations on reckless bike and moped riders   
- Install speed cameras in school and senior zones    
- Increase penalties for dangerous driving behaviors    
- Create dedicated bike lanes with proper enforcement
### Animal Control and Public Safety
- Crack down on dangerous and unattended dogs    
- Strengthen leash laws and owner accountability    
- Expand animal control officer presence    
- Create safe neighborhood walking programs for seniors
### Gun Violence Prevention
- Remove illegal guns through focused community policing 
- Expand gun buyback programs with community partnerships    
- Target repeat offenders with enhanced prosecution    
- Support violence interruption programs in high-risk area    
## Criminal Justice Reform
### Police Department Reform
- Community- based policing in every ward    
- Body camera requirements for all officers    
- Enhanced training on de-escalation and mental health    
- Accountability measures for officer misconduct
### Court System Improvements
- Faster processing of violent crime cases 
- Victim support services and advocacy programs    
- Drug court expansion for non-violent offenders    
- Community service alternatives for minor violations
### Correctional System Reform
- Focus on rehabilitation and job training programs 
- Mental health and addiction treatment services
- Reentry support to reduce recidivism rates
- Family visitation programs to maintain community ties﻿
## Community-Driven Safety

### Neighborhood Watch Programs
- Expand resident-led safety initiatives    
- Provide training and resources for community leaders    
- Coordinate with police for effective partnerships    
- Monthly safety meetings in all eight wards   
### Senior Safety Priority
- Enhanced patrols around senior housing and centers 
- Emergency response systems for elderly residents
- Safe transportation programs for medical appointments
- Community escort services for essential errands
### Youth Intervention Programs
- After-school programs in high-crime areas    
- Mentorship and job training for at-risk youth    
- Sports and recreational opportunities    
- Conflict resolution and leadership training
