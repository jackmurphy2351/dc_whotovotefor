# About
I'm a father, husband, veteran, and proud public servant who came from humble beginnings. I was born in foster care and raised in a home that relied on food stamps and social safety net programs. I remember the feeling of standing in line with my parents to recertify for benefits—the looks, the judgment, and the lack of dignity we were afforded. I told myself that if I ever had the chance to work in government, I'd serve in a way that helped people hold onto their dignity, not lose it.

Service before self is a value that has guided me throughout my life—from the military, where I learned to put mission and community first, to my work in education and government. My mother was the neighborhood caregiver, the one who made sure everyone had a warm meal and a place to feel safe. She taught me the power of community and the importance of never losing hope.

### Supporting Small Businesses
Small businesses are the backbone of our neighborhoods and local economy. On the DC Council, I will work to expand access to capital for entrepreneurs, strengthen commercial corridors, and reduce unnecessary barriers that make it harder for local businesses to start and grow. ### Protecting DC's Local Control
The District must be able to govern itself responsibly. I will work with city leadership to defend DC's ability to manage its own budget, protect locally raised tax dollars, and ensure our residents—not outside interests—shape the future of our city.
### Responsible Budget Leadership
The DC Council controls how our local dollars are invested. I will support responsible budgeting that protects essential services, invests in housing and workforce development, and ensures taxpayers see real results from every public dollar spent.
### Education and Opportunity for Youth
As President of the DC State Board of Education, I have spent years working with families, educators, and students to strengthen our schools. On the Council, I will continue advocating for strong public schools, workforce pathways for young people, and programs that prepare students for college, careers, and leadership in our city.
### Safer Communities Through Prevention
Public safety requires both strong policing and strong communities. I support expanding programs that prevent youth violence by investing in education, mentorship, workforce opportunities, and neighborhood-based initiatives that give young people a path toward success.

### My Small Business Action Plan
#### Access to Capital
Too many DC small businesses still struggle to access the capital they need to start, grow, and compete. As a member of the DC Council, I will work to strengthen the District's small business financing ecosystem by expanding support for Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs), increasing funding for DC-based small business grant and loan programs, and ensuring our local entrepreneurs—especially minority- and women-owned businesses—have fair access to capital.

I will also work with local banks, credit unions, and economic development partners to expand lending opportunities and ensure District resources are reaching businesses in every ward, particularly in communities that have historically lacked investment.
#### Workforce Development
The District must prepare residents for the jobs shaping our regional economy. On the DC Council, I will support stronger partnerships between the Workforce Investment Council, local employers, community colleges, and training providers to expand career pathways for DC residents.

This includes investing in apprenticeship and credential programs that connect residents directly to growing industries such as technology, healthcare, infrastructure, and clean energy. We must also ensure that workforce programs prioritize residents who have been historically left out of opportunity, including returning citizens and veterans.

>When we invest in people, we strengthen the entire city.
#### Affordable Commercial Space
Small businesses are the backbone of our neighborhoods, but rising rents are making it harder for them to stay rooted in the communities they serve. As a DC Councilmember, I will support policies that preserve and expand affordable commercial space so local businesses can grow and thrive.

This includes encouraging mixed-use development that includes neighborhood-serving retail, supporting programs that help small businesses secure long-term leases, and working with planning and economic development agencies to ensure commercial corridors remain vibrant and accessible to local entrepreneurs.

>Protecting neighborhood businesses means protecting the character and economic stability of our communities.
#### Smart and Streamlined Regulations
Starting and running a business in DC should be easier, not harder. I will work to modernize the District's small business regulatory process by simplifying licensing requirements, reducing unnecessary delays, and ensuring agencies provide clear guidance and support to entrepreneurs.

The goal is to make DC a city where small businesses can launch, grow, and succeed without being buried in bureaucracy. Our local government should be a partner in innovation and entrepreneurship, helping businesses navigate regulations while maintaining strong standards that protect workers and consumers.
