## Ward 1 City Council Campaign Of Terry Lynch Calls For Immediate Parking Relief For Ward 1  Residents
### Ward 1 Residents Need Parking Relief Now
The Campaign of Ward 1 City Council Candidate Terry Lynch has called on city officials and the DC City Council to enact steps to provide immediate relief to frustrated Ward 1 residents who are in need of parking.

“I hear all the time from residents that they cannot find parking”, says Lynch. “I see it – out of state cars park with impunity in spaces reserved for Ward 1 residents, or parking days on end in what are 2 hour parking spaces meant for turnover for possible parking for residents and others”, he says. “I believe Ward 1 residents are losing out on literally hundreds of parking spaces every day to out of state parking violators. To that end, the city should immediately take the following actions:

– Extend Zone 1 Parking restrictions to 7 days a week, 24 hours a day. This would at least provide parking on one side of blocks to DC residents.

– Make violators of Zone 1 parking restrictions eligible for immediate towing. 

– Expand parking enforcement to include Sundays. 

In my first hundred days I will introduce legislation for this if the city has not acted. Ward 1 residents need relief and they need it now. Just yesterday I saw an out of state vehicle with numerous tickets on it dating back to 2024 parked in the same 2 hour location for months – preventing it from being used by residents. That’s nuts and should not be happening”, says Lynch. “Further if we are serious about curbing violence we must have Sunday parking enforcement especially in areas where drug trafficking is happening, Drug trafficking does not take Sundays off”, says Lynch.

## ENFORCE PARKING IN ColHi, FREE UP SPACE FOR WARD ONE RESIDENTS
MOUNT PLEASANT, DC – Longtime District Activist and Ward One DC Council candidate Terry Lynch of Mount Pleasant (D), today urged more vigorous parking enforcement in Columbia Heights, DC in an effort to free up parking spaces for Ward One residents.

“I have asked parking enforcement to do twice-a-day walk-throughs of Columbia Heights, as the Zone 1 parking restrictions are widely ignored nor enforced,” remarked Terry Lynch.

“Just yesterday there were eight vehicles with out-of-state plates – without District-issued visitor parking passes – in Zone 1 parking spaces along the 1400 blocks of Girard and Fairmont Sts. NW alone. These vehicles were taking away spaces from Ward one residents,” reported Lynch.

Further, Lynch reported, “I am also seeing out-of-state plates parked in no parking zones on these blocks. I came across two alone this week – each with over 90 outstanding tickets – most for speeding.

“It is the Wild West with regards to parking right now in Columbia Heights and we can make it safer and better with proper enforcement.” vowed Lynch.
