## We don't collect your data

Help Me Vote DC 2026! does not collect, store, or share personal information. Here's exactly what happens when you use this site:

**No database.** There is no server-side database. Nothing about you is saved anywhere other than your own browser.

**No analytics or trackers.** There are no Google Analytics scripts, advertising pixels, social sharing widgets, or third-party tracking of any kind.

**No third-party CDN or fonts.** All CSS, JavaScript, and fonts are served directly from this site. There are no calls to Google Fonts, jsDelivr, or any other external CDN that could log your IP address.

**Quiz answers live only in a signed cookie on your device.** When you answer quiz questions, your responses are stored in an encrypted, tamper-proof session cookie in your browser. They are *never* transmitted to or stored on the server — the server sees only a signed blob it uses to render your results. If you clear your browser cookies or the session expires, your answers are gone.

**No accounts.** You never create an account, enter an email address, or provide any identifying information.

## How to clear your quiz answers

To reset your answers for any race, use the **Reset my answers** link on the quiz page. To clear everything at once, delete this site's cookies in your browser settings.
